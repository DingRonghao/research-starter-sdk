# Apparatus and protocol

- 635 nm diode source, synthetic example identifier RS-635
- Rotating analyzer, 15 degree step
- Five repeated readings at each angle
- Blocked-source detector offset: 0.030 normalized units
- Linear-state preparation angle: approximately 30 degrees
- Circular-state preparation: quarter-wave plate nominally at 45 degrees

## Quality checks

Record analyzer zero, detector saturation status, blocked-source offset, and repeat-to-repeat spread before interpreting visibility.
